## Approved MVP architecture

Owner approval: 2026-09-19.

- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript running entirely in the browser.
- No backend, database, authentication, framework, external API, or paid service.
- Browser Web Audio may be used for the completion notification.
- Playwright is approved as a test-only development dependency.
- The user-facing app must remain deployable as a static site.

Any backend, database, authentication, payment system, external API, paid service, framework, or additional dependency requires new owner approval.
